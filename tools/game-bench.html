Rules

    Ports

    The page drives clk from its clock and holds rst_n at 0 while R is down, and both rst_n and clr_n at 0 while C is down (each for at least 3 ticks, and both when the console switches on). A key press is held until at least one rising clock edge has seen it. LEDs light on 1. Segment outputs a–g drive a common-anode display, so a segment lights on 0 (connect them straight from a 7447).

    Probe wires

    Pick any wire in your circuit to watch it. Traces show the last 48 clock half-periods, newest on the right.

    Chip library

    These are the only modules you may use. Connect pins by name, like .A1(x). Every input pin must be connected; tie unused ones to 1'b0 or 1'b1. Unused outputs can be left out. Pins ending in _n are active low. To test in iverilog, compile your file together with chips.v.
